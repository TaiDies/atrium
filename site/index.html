Everything in one place

Write, plan, talk and meet — together

Replace a stack of separate tools with one workspace that your whole team shares in real time.

Documents

A block editor with to-dos, callouts, toggles, columns, equations, embeds, comments, page history and live cursors.

Databases

Grids, boards, calendars, timelines, galleries, charts and forms — with relations, rollups, formulas and automations.

Calendar & Today

Events, tasks, reminders and repeats; every dated card and to-do in one calendar; plan your day hour by hour.

Chat

Private chats, groups and teams with per-member rules, @mentions, page links, dates and slash commands.

Meetings

Scheduled links, invites, screen sharing, host controls, prepared tasks and a meeting chat that's kept afterwards.

Knowledge graph

See how every page connects, Obsidian-style — with colour groups that mean something and an animated legend.

AI that builds

Bring Claude, OpenAI-compatible or Gemini models: an assistant that makes documents, databases and plans, plus autofill, flashcards and transcription.

Files & Office

Edit documents, spreadsheets and presentations in built-in editors, write on PDFs, sketch on whiteboards, and search inside every file.

API, webhooks & MCP

Tokens, a documented API, signed webhooks — and Connect Claude gives an AI assistant a workspace as its memory.

Yours, completely

Self-hosted, private, unlimited

  • Your server, your data. Everything lives in your own database and storage.
  • No per-seat limits. As many people and workspaces as your machine can hold — you set the limits.
  • Pages you've opened work offline. They stay on the device and sync when you're back.
  • Imports everything. Notion, Evernote, Trello, documents, Markdown, CSV — or pages written by any AI chat, files and all.
  • English, Spanish, French and Arabic (right-to-left), themes that follow your account to every device, and a phone layout that feels native.
  • Updates in one click. The admin panel tells you when a new version is out — or installs it automatically.
# one machine with Docker is all you need
$ git clone https://github.com/TaiDies/atrium.git
$ cd atrium && cp .env.example .env
$ docker compose up -d --build
# open http://localhost:3000 — done
Coming later

Atrium Cloud — no server needed

Down the line, we'll offer Atrium as a hosted subscription for teams who'd rather not run their own server: the same app, kept up to date and backed up for you, with the Windows and Android apps connecting to it out of the box.

  • Self-hosting stays free and fully featured, forever — open source under the AGPL.
  • Move either way. Export your workspace from the cloud to your own server, or the other way round.
  • Want to hear first? Watch the GitHub repository for the announcement.
